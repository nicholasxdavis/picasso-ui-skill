  • What does Acme actually do?

    We plan, install and run charging for commercial fleets: depot chargers, on-route hubs and the software that ties them together. Our platform schedules every session around your routes and energy tariffs, so vehicles leave charged and your site stays within its power limits.

  • Which vehicles and chargers do you support?

    Mixed fleets are the norm for us: vans, box trucks, buses and pool cars on CCS1, CCS2, NACS or Type 2 connectors. Our software works with any OCPP 1.6 or 2.0.1 compliant charger, so you can keep hardware you already own and add to it over time.

  • How long does a depot installation take?

    A typical 20-vehicle depot runs 10 to 14 weeks from site survey to first charge. Most of that is utility approval, which we manage for you along with permits, civil works and commissioning. Sites with spare grid capacity can move considerably faster.

  • Will smart charging lower our energy bill?

    That's what it's built for. Sessions shift into off-peak windows and site load is capped to avoid demand charges, while every vehicle still reaches its target state of charge before departure. You see the cost of each session per vehicle in your dashboard.

  • What happens when a charger goes offline?

    Every port is monitored around the clock. Most faults are cleared with a remote reset, and the platform reroutes vehicles to free ports in the meantime. If a repair needs hands on site, a technician is dispatched within the response time set in your service plan.

  • How is pricing structured?

    You can buy hardware outright and add a software subscription, or choose Charging-as-a-Service: one monthly fee per port that covers hardware, installation, software and maintenance, with no upfront capital outlay.

  • Can drivers charge at home or on the road?

    Yes. Home sessions are metered and reimbursed automatically through the driver app, and public charging across major networks is billed to a single Acme card. Depot, home and public charging all land on one monthly invoice.
